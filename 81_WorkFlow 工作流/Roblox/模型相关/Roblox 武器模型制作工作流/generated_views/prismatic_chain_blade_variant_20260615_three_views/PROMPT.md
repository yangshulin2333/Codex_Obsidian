## Design Lock

```text

Keep the same weapon family identity: a vertical oversized black obsidian rectangular slab blade, long straight blade body with a sharp diamond-like lower tip, short upright cylindrical grip above the blade, a chain attached near the top shoulder, and a small black triangular dangling blade charm hanging from the chain on the upper right side.
Keep the bright rainbow energy edge running down the blade sides, with cyan near the top, green/yellow along the middle, and orange/red near the bottom tip.
Make only moderate variant changes: make the blade slightly wider at the shoulders, add clean dark charcoal inset panels on the black center area, add a circular low-poly emblem near the upper blade, add a few subtle rivets and thin vertical energy grooves, and make the dangling chain charm a clearer faceted triangular spike.
The front, side, and back images must describe the exact same weapon design.

Use a clean Roblox low-poly readable silhouette with hand-painted texture detail, not dense high-poly sculpture.
Details should look painted into the texture or simple flat bevel planes, optimized for about 3000 to 4000 triangles.
```
