## Design Lock

Create one same-series redesigned Roblox fantasy polearm weapon based on the provided reference screenshots.

Keep the same weapon family identity, total height, long handle proportion, head size, blade placement, guard position, pommel position, and decorative element placement in every view.

Slightly reshape the crescent blade so it has a sharper inner hook and a cleaner wider white cutting edge. Simplify the ornate black-and-gold patterns into painted texture motifs suitable for Roblox low-poly.

Low-poly constraints: low-poly Roblox fantasy weapon, clean flat planes, game-ready low-poly mesh, details painted in texture, not sculpted geometry, simple solid silhouette, no sculpted high-poly relief, no dense geometry details, no tiny detached parts, no floating fragments, no noisy surface, no hair-like spikes, no small 3D cracks, no random blocks, optimized visually for about 3000 to 4000 triangles.
