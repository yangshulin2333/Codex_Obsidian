```text

vertical handle, horizontal hammer head, conical spike on one side, icy stone/metal body,
white frost/bone bands, and painted weathering.

Show one single consistent weapon design in exactly three orthographic views:
left side profile, front view, and right side view.

bands, pommel, and texture pattern.
```
