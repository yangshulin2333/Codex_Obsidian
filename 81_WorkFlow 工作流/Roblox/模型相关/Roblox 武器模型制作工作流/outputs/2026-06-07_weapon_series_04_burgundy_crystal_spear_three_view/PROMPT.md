# weapon_series_04_burgundy_crystal_spear 三视图 Prompt

用途：给 Meshy / 3D AI 做 Roblox 低模武器参考图。

## 生成目标

同系列改款，不完全复制原参考图。

保留系列特征：

1. 长竖向武器轮廓。
2. 深色分段金属杆身。
3. 上方黑色端头。
4. 银灰色螺旋缠绕握把。
5. 下方红色刀刃/枪刃。

本次改款：

1. 红色改成深酒红、暗玫瑰红、水晶红。
2. 下方武器头改成更稳定的红晶枪刃。
3. 护手改成紧凑对称肩甲。
4. 结构保持低模清晰大轮廓，细节主要画在贴图里。

## 核心 Prompt

```text
Create one clean orthographic three-view weapon reference sheet, showing the same redesigned weapon in three vertical views: left side view, front view, right side view. Place the three views evenly side by side on one canvas.

A low-poly Roblox-style fantasy spear-sword hybrid weapon. It has a long slim dark gunmetal segmented shaft, a dark rounded pommel at the top, a silver-gray spiral wrapped grip near the upper third, simple metal rings between shaft sections, and a lower spear-dagger blade. The lower blade is redesigned into a cleaner faceted crimson crystal spear blade with a compact symmetrical shoulder guard. Keep the family feel of the reference weapon but change the variant shape slightly.

Use similar colors to the reference, but shifted: deep burgundy red and dark rose-crimson blade, cool blackened steel, weathered gunmetal gray, pale silver-gray spiral wrap, subtle desaturated blue-gray highlights. No bright gold.

Clean 3D concept render, low-poly Roblox game asset design, flat readable planes, hand-painted texture detail, game-ready look, about 3000 to 4000 triangle complexity.

Orthographic front/side model sheet, full weapon visible from top pommel to blade tip, centered vertically, large enough to inspect, equal scale across all three views, generous padding, no perspective distortion.

Plain light gray studio background, no floor horizon, no UI, no axes, no labels, no text.

The three views must clearly represent the same weapon with matching proportions, same ring positions, same grip length, same blade length, same top pommel size. Keep the silhouette clean and stable for 3D reconstruction. Details should be painted into texture, not modeled as dense geometry.
```

## 反向约束

```text
no high-poly sculpted relief, no dense geometry details, no tiny detached parts, no floating fragments, no noisy surface, no hair-like spikes, no small 3D cracks, no complex skull sculpture, no dense filigree, no random blocks, no character holding it, no background props, no watermark
```
