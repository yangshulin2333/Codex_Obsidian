建议所有角色、道具和地形模型统一使用下面这段视角约束：

> 严格的左侧正交视图。摄像机固定在物体正左侧，与物体侧面完全垂直，水平旋转角度恰好为90度。仅使用一个固定摄像机和单一观察方向，不是左前方或左后方的三分之四视图。使用正交投影，不使用透视投影。画面只能展示从严格左侧实际可见的表面，所有前后对齐的结构必须自然重叠，远侧结构应被近侧结构遮挡。不得为了展示完整结构而移动、错开或展开被遮挡的部件。

对应的英文建议保留，因为很多生成模型对相机术语的英文理解更稳定：

> **Strict orthographic left-side view at exactly 90 degrees. Use one fixed camera and a single viewing direction. The camera is positioned directly on the object's left side and perpendicular to its side plane. Orthographic projection, no perspective. Show only surfaces genuinely visible from the exact left side. Near-side parts must naturally occlude aligned far-side parts. Do not shift, stagger, spread, rotate, or reveal hidden parts for visibility.**

负面提示词建议固定加：

> three-quarter view, left-front view, left-rear view, perspective view, multiple viewpoints, multi-angle composition, isometric view, visible front face, visible rear face, rotated object, offset parts, staggered parts, separated overlapping parts, intentionally revealed hidden parts, exploded view, wide-angle lens, perspective distortion



可以固定记这一句：

> **严格左侧正交视图，单摄像机，正对物体左侧，禁止透视和三分之四视角，遮挡部分不要刻意露出。**

英文版通常识别更稳定：

> **Strict orthographic left-side view, single fixed camera, no perspective or three-quarter angle, hidden parts remain occluded.**

生成其他方向时只替换方向词：

- 左视图：`left-side view`
- 右视图：`right-side view`
- 正视图：`front view`
- 后视图：`rear view`
- 顶视图：`top view`

你主要记住三个词就够了：

> **Strict + Orthographic + Single camera**

意思就是：**严格、正交、单摄像机。**
