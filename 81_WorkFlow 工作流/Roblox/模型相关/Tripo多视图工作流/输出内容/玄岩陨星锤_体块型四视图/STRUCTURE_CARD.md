## Volume Lock

- Main head: bulky short round drum / meteor-stone cylinder, not a flat disk.
- Head material: dark charcoal faceted stone with hand-painted cracks and worn low-poly facets.
- Bands: two large vertical cold-iron bands visible on the front, wrapping around the head volume.
- Side knobs: broad blunt faceted knobs distributed around the rim; side-facing knobs must remain visible in side views.
- Front mark: simple blue-gray double-chevron rune on the front face; it may be hidden or reduced on side/back views.
- Top cap: short faceted dark/iron cap centered on top of the head.
- Lower collar: pale square metal collar block directly under the head.
- Shaft: long straight dark handle with simple faceted sides and a few silver metal rings.
- Bottom cap: small pale faceted metal end cap.

## View Rules

- Back view: same round head volume, bands, side knobs, top cap, lower collar, handle, and bottom cap; rear face may have a simpler rune or plain stone face.
- Left/right side views: true 90-degree side views with bulky round drum volume, not a thin line.
- Side views must preserve side-facing blunt knobs, metal band thickness, top cap, lower collar, handle alignment, and bottom cap.
- Do not add chains, blades, axe heads, sharp spike arrays, loose ball segments, hands, character, UI, text, or watermark.
