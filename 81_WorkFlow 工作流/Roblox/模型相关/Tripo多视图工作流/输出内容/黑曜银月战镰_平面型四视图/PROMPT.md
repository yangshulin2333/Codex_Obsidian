## Back Prompt

Using the accepted front-view weapon image as the exact design lock, generate ONE independent BACK VIEW image of the same Roblox low-poly fantasy war scythe.

It must look like the same model rotated 180 degrees, not a new weapon. Preserve the same total height, very long straight handle, bottom wrapped grip and pointed spike, main crescent blade length and width, top hook blade, compact head socket, slim slanted connector rib with open negative space, obsidian black + cold silver + subtle blue-steel accent palette, faceted low-poly planes, hand-painted metal material, and simple Roblox viewport/model screenshot feel.

The rear side may have simpler painted detail and less visible blue accent, but the major silhouette and material language must match the accepted front view exactly. Keep the scythe head structure plausible from the back: same blade root, same socket position, same lower handle alignment.

Full weapon visible, centered vertical orthographic BACK view, clean light Roblox viewport-style background, generous padding around the weapon.
No text, no watermark, no UI, no character, no hand, no new parts, no extra side spikes, no serrations, no saw teeth, no bulky triangular filler plate, no dangling charm, no chain, no high-poly ornament, no flat vector style, no 3/4 angle.

## Left Side Prompt

Using the accepted front-view weapon image as the exact design lock, generate ONE independent LEFT 90-DEGREE SIDE VIEW image of the same Roblox low-poly fantasy war scythe.

This must be a true side profile, not a 3/4 view. This is a flat long-handled scythe weapon: compress the front-facing crescent blade, top hook blade, head socket, and slanted connector rib into a narrow side edge. The blade/head should appear thin from the side, with only side-visible thickness, beveled edge planes, and a compact socket silhouette.

Preserve the same total height, very long straight handle, bottom wrapped grip and pointed spike, head position near the top, obsidian black + cold silver + subtle blue-steel accent palette, faceted low-poly Roblox model screenshot feel, simple lighting, and clean viewport background. The small blue accent may appear only as a tiny thin side sliver or rivet edge, not a full front-facing gem.

Full weapon visible, centered vertical orthographic LEFT 90-degree side view, clean light Roblox viewport-style background, generous padding.
No text, no watermark, no UI, no character, no hand, no 3/4 angle, no broad front-facing crescent blade, no front-facing guard spread, no new geometry, no extra side spikes, no serrations, no saw teeth, no dangling charm, no chain, no bulky triangular plate, no high-poly ornament, no flat vector style.
