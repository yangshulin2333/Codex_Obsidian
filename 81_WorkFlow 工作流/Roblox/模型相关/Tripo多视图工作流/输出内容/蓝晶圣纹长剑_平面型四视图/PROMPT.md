Using the accepted front-view weapon image as the exact design lock, generate ONE independent BACK VIEW image of the same Roblox low-poly fantasy royal longsword.

It must look like the same model rotated 180 degrees, not a new weapon. Preserve the same total height, long straight double-edged silver blade, pointed diamond tip, broad blade width, gold stepped royal guard/collar width, central tall sapphire shield gem position, two smaller side gem positions, blue-and-gold segmented upper handle, pale ivory wrapped grip cap, black-and-gold central blade inlay placement, color palette, material texture, silhouette, and Roblox viewport/model screenshot feel.

The rear texture may be simpler and less gem-forward, but the major shape, gold guard/collar, blade inlay route, handle bands, blade proportions, and sapphire/gold/silver material language must match the accepted front view. The back side may show rear gem settings or simplified raised metal backs, but no new weapon parts.

Full weapon visible, centered vertical orthographic BACK VIEW, clean light Roblox viewport-style background, generous padding.
