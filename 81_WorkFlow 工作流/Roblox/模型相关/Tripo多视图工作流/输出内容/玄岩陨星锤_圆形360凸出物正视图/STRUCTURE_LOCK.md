## Required Weapon Logic

- The hammer head is a large round / spherical stone mace head.
- The head must not read as a flat disk, pancake, short barrel, or narrow drum.
- Protrusions are distributed around the full 360-degree spherical head, not only on the left and right silhouette.
- The visible front face includes multiple broad blunt raised studs.
- The rim includes side-visible studs that imply the same pattern continues to the left, right, top, bottom, and rear side of the sphere.
- All protrusions are embedded into the stone surface and follow the sphere surface direction.
- Protrusions must be broad, blunt, faceted low-poly knobs; they are not thin sharp spikes.

## Front View Readability

- The front view should show a central front stud and several surrounding front-face studs.
- The left and right edge studs show the side ring of protrusions.
- The top and lower visible edge may show partial studs to imply vertical wraparound.
- Metal bands may remain as structural rings, but they must not hide the 360-degree knob distribution.

## Four-View Rules After Approval

- Back view: show the same spherical head and a matching rear stud pattern.
- Left side view: preserve full spherical head volume and side-facing stud rows.
- Right side view: mirror or match the accepted left side if the structure is symmetrical.
- Side views must not collapse the head into a thin line or flat oval.
- No chains, loose balls, blades, axe heads, sharp spike arrays, hands, characters, UI, text, or watermark.

## Acceptance Gate

- round head: required
- 360-degree protrusion logic: required
- physically plausible attachment to sphere: required
- Roblox low-poly viewport feel: required
